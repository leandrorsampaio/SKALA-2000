A chrome bat-handle toggle on a brushed aluminium plate with four screws, a hex nut and the words ON and OFF engraved above and below: up is ON. The console has one, MAINS on panel E, paired with a green POWER ON window.

**The lever swings toward the operator, not around the panel.** Seen from the front, a toggle's lever never rotates like a clock hand: it foreshortens. The throw is a vertical scale of the lever about the pivot from +1 (up) through 0 (pointing straight at you, when only its round tip is seen end-on over the bushing) to −1 (down), in 100 ms with a slow-fast-slow curve, its shadow flipping with it. The `clunk`, the loudest on the console, lands 60 ms in, as the lever passes centre.

**Power-down:** every lamp decays with filament timing, nixies go dark instantly, needles fall to the left stop over 900 ms, drum counters keep their reading. **Power-up sequence:** POWER ON window after 250 ms; nixies strike one row at a time, 40 ms apart, each showing all eights for 120 ms; needles rise together; then an automatic 1 s lamp test; then live data. In the app, MAINS OFF means the console stops polling and shows nothing; it does not quit the app.

**Consumer provides:** label, state, change handler.

**Depth.** The aluminium plate is raised (drop shadow), the nut and lever each cast their own shadow onto it.
